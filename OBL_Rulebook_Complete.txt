OBL RULEBOOK — COMPLETE, FINAL VERSION

1.  Field & Equipment 1.1 The field shall include clearly marked zones:

-   20-Foot Line (minimum fair-hit distance)
-   Far-Zone Boundary (100+ feet from home)
-   Ground-Limit Boundary (designated area where the ball may bounce
    once)

1.2 All measurements must be agreed upon by both players before the
match begins.

2.  Gameplay Structure 2.1 A match consists of 5 periods. 2.2 A period
    ends only when both players have completed one turn as pitcher and
    one turn as batter. 2.3 After every out, pitcher and batter switch
    positions immediately. 2.4 If both players have already played both
    roles within a period, the period ends and the next begins.

3.  Batting Rules 3.1 Bunting is prohibited. Any bunt results in an
    immediate out. 3.2 A hit that does not travel at least 20 feet in
    fair territory is a strike. 3.3 A hit into the Far Zone (100+ feet)
    awards 2 points. 3.4 Scoring Limit Rule:

-   Once a batter scores 8 total points within a period, all additional
    hits only award 1 point.
-   Scoring resets to normal at the start of the next period.

4.  Pitching & Strikes/Balls 4.1 A strike is any pitch that enters the
    strike zone and is not hit fairly. 4.2 A ball is any pitch that
    fails to enter the strike zone. 4.3 Four balls result in 1 point
    awarded to the batter, then the count resets.

5.  Ball-in-Play Rules 5.1 If the pitcher catches the hit ball before it
    touches the ground, the batter is out. 5.2 Ground-Limit Rule:

-   The ball may bounce once inside the ground-limit area.
-   If it bounces more than once, the batter is out and players switch
    roles. 5.3 Disputes about bounces must be resolved by replaying the
    pitch.

6.  Outs & Switching 6.1 Any out causes an immediate switch. 6.2 If
    switching would cause a player to repeat the same role twice in a
    period, the period ends.

7.  Conduct & Penalties 7.1 A sportsmanship violation results in:

-   Loss of 2 points, and
-   Immediate role switch. 7.2 Repeated violations may result in removal
    from the match if agreed beforehand.

8.  Definitions

-   Strike Zone: A mutually agreed zone before play begins.
-   Bounce: Any ground contact where the ball changes direction or comes
    to rest.
-   Bunt: Any hit where the batter intentionally deadens the ball or
    uses a check-swing producing minimal forward motion.

9.  Dispute Resolution 9.1 Replay the pitch for any unresolved
    disagreement. 9.2 Scores revert to the last mutually agreed total if
    a scoring dispute occurs.

10. Overtime Rules 10.1 Overtime begins if the score is tied after 5
    periods.

10.2 Structure of Overtime 10.2.1 Overtime uses OT Periods, identical in
structure to normal periods. 10.2.2 Each OT Period ends only after each
player has pitched once and batted once. 10.2.3 Unlimited OT Periods may
be played until a winner is determined.

10.3 Overtime Objective 10.3.1 Each player must score +3 points above
the score they entered OT with. 10.3.2 The OT target score does not
increase within the same OT Period. 10.3.3 When a player reaches their
+3 target, they immediately switch roles.

10.4 Determining the Winner 10.4.1 If only one player reaches their +3
target within an OT Period, they win immediately. 10.4.2 If both players
reach their +3 target, the OT Period ends and another begins. 10.4.3 In
the new OT Period, each player must again reach +3 above their previous
ending score.

10.5 Example - Game ends tied 10–10. - OT Period 1: Both must reach
13. - If both do, OT Period 2: Both must reach 16. - Repeat until one
player reaches their +3 target and the other does not.

10.6 Special OT Clarifications 10.6.1 Overshooting the target still
triggers immediate switching at the moment the target is crossed. 10.6.2
Ball/strike counts reset when a player reaches their target. 10.6.3
Sportsmanship rules still apply and may affect the required target.
10.6.4 All normal gameplay rules remain active unless stated otherwise.

END OF RULEBOOK
